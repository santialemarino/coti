# Customer portal and quotation requests

Customers open their supplier's web address and choose **Continue with Google**, or sign in
with CUIT/CUIL and a password. First-time Google users supply their CUIT/CUIL once. Password
registration accepts an optional recovery email, which must be verified before it can recover
access. Email verification does not block requesting a quotation.

A customer session lasts 30 days by default. Returning customers see **My requests**, with a
request's date, branch and state, and actions to create another request or sign out. The history
includes earlier requests from any intake channel when their client profile or retained web
request carries the same CUIT/CUIL within that supplier. Requests without that identifier cannot
be inferred from a name or an unrelated contact.

To request a quotation, customers first choose a branch, then enter their name or business name.
Email and phone are optional. Their authenticated CUIT/CUIL is already filled and cannot be
changed in the request form. They choose between writing an order and uploading one image, PDF,
spreadsheet, audio recording or text file. The receipt screen confirms durable reception.

The seller receives the original request and its customer declarations. This flow does not create,
merge or modify commercial client profiles. The seller confirms the client association while sending the quotation.

Customers can consult and answer seller-sent quotations from their history or an existing delivery
link; both open the same authenticated detail. Internal quotation drafts remain private. When
sending, the seller still supplies the required WhatsApp recipient. Sending also publishes the
quotation in the customer portal automatically, including when the external delivery fails.

Password recovery asks for CUIT/CUIL and the account's verified recovery email. A matching request
receives a one-use reset link; resetting the password closes previous sessions.
