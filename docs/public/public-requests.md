# Requesting a quote through a public link

A customer opens the supplier's branch link without creating an account. Each request starts with
an empty form asking for CUIT, name or business name, email, and an optional phone number.

The next screen offers two ways to send an order: write a materials list, or upload one image,
PDF, spreadsheet, audio recording, or text file. The customer can return to edit their contact
details before sending. The form shows receipt once the request has been stored.

The request enters the corresponding branch's seller queue with its original content and the
customer's declarations. The seller reviews the extracted materials before quoting. Customer
declarations do not expose or automatically change existing client profiles; the seller confirms
the association through the accepted-sale workflow.

The receipt screen confirms submission only. Reviewing and responding to a seller-approved quote
continues through the separate quote link the seller sends afterward.
