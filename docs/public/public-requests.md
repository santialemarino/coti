# Customer portal and quotation requests

Customers open their supplier's web address and choose **Continue with Google**, or sign in
with CUIT/CUIL and a password. First-time Google users supply their CUIT/CUIL once. Verifying a
contact does not block requesting a quotation.

A customer session lasts 30 days by default. Returning customers see **My requests**, with a
request's date, branch and state, and actions to create another request or sign out. The history
includes earlier requests from any intake channel when their client profile or retained web
request carries the same CUIT/CUIL within that supplier. Requests without that identifier cannot
be inferred from a name or an unrelated contact.

To request a quotation, customers first choose a branch, then enter their name or business name.
Email and phone are optional. Their authenticated CUIT/CUIL is already filled and cannot be
changed in the request form. They choose between writing an order and uploading one image, PDF,
spreadsheet, audio recording or text file. The receipt screen confirms durable reception.

The seller receives the original request and its customer declarations. Authenticated declarations
can create or enrich the customer's own commercial profile. The seller confirms the request's
client association while sending the quotation. Customers can manage their optional phones and
emails from **My data**, and choose which verified one is their main contact.

Customers can consult and answer seller-sent quotations from their history or an existing delivery
link; both open the same authenticated detail. Internal quotation drafts remain private. When
sending, the seller selects WhatsApp, email, or both and supplies the corresponding destinations.
Sending also publishes the quotation in the customer portal automatically, including when the
external delivery fails.

Password recovery asks for the CUIT/CUIL, then lists the account's verified contacts, masked, with
the main one preselected. The customer picks one, enters the one-use code it receives and a new
password, and is signed in; previous sessions close. Without a verified contact there is no
recovery. Codes by WhatsApp wait for Meta's authentication template, so until then only email
contacts can receive one.
