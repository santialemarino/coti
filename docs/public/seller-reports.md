# Seller reports

The Reports section summarizes activity assigned to the signed-in seller. It includes received
orders, sent and accepted quotes, the current quote status breakdown, the most frequently requested
catalog products, and the seller's most active clients.

The conversion rate is accepted quotes divided by sent quotes. Average quote time measures the
elapsed time from an RFQ's receipt to the first time its quote was sent. Reports can be filtered by
the RFQ receipt date; leaving both date fields blank includes the seller's full available history.

Data is limited to the signed-in seller and the branches they can access. When a branch is selected,
the report is limited to that branch.
